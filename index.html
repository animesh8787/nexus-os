Privacy

Built local-first,
on purpose.

A job search is personal. Nexus only touches what a feature needs, and tells you exactly what that is.

  • Your data stays yoursYour tracker, profile and solutions live in your browser under your account — not in a shared database. Export or delete them any time.
  • Gmail is read-only and narrowNexus searches for job-related mail and reads only the sender, subject, date and a short snippet. The access token is never saved.
  • Your resume goes nowhere by surpriseResume text is sent to the AI provider only when you press Parse, and nothing is applied to your profile until you confirm it.
  • No auto-apply, no job-site passwordsNexus never submits applications for you and never asks for your logins on other sites.
Questions

Good things to ask.

Does Nexus OS apply to jobs for me?
No. Apply opens the employer's own application page, where you upload your resume and answer their questions. Nexus records the application afterwards — it never submits anything on your behalf.
How does an application reach the tracker on its own?
Two ways. When you return from an employer's page, Nexus asks whether you applied and logs it in one tap. And if you connect Gmail, the confirmation email each employer sends is matched to the role automatically, with the date.
Which email providers are supported?
Gmail, through Google's read-only access. Nexus looks for job-related messages only. You can disconnect at any time from Settings.
Do I need my own AI key?
No. The AI features run through a shared service with fair-use limits. If you prefer, you can plug in your own free Groq key in Settings instead.
Where is my data stored?
In your browser, namespaced to your account. There is a one-click JSON export and a reset in Settings. Sign-in itself is handled by Firebase Authentication.
Is it free?
Nexus OS is free while it is in early access.
