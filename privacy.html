2. Gmail (only if you choose to connect it)

  • Nexus OS asks Google for read-only access (gmail.readonly) so it can find job-related emails — application confirmations, assessment and interview invitations, offers and rejections — and record them on your tracker.
  • It searches your mailbox for job-related messages, and for each match reads only the sender, subject, date and the short snippet Gmail provides. It does not read full message bodies or attachments, and it never sends, deletes or changes email.
  • By default, reading and classifying happens in your browser. The access token lasts about an hour and is kept in memory only; it is never written to storage or sent to our servers.
  • What is saved to your tracker: the company, role, event type and date, the email's subject line, and Gmail's message ID (to avoid recording the same email twice). This is part of your portal data (section 1) and is deleted with it.
  • Optional AI check for unclear emails (off by default). Nexus first sorts emails with its own rules, in your browser. If you turn on “AI check for unclear emails” in the Job Tracker, then for emails those rules could not place — at most 20 per inbox check — Nexus sends the sender's name and domain (not the email address), the subject line and Gmail's short snippet to our AI service (a Cloudflare Worker), which forwards it to Groq. If you use your own Groq key instead, it is sent from your browser straight to Groq. Groq's answer says whether each email looks like a confirmation, assessment, interview, offer or rejection, and which company and role it concerns. Nexus never sends full message bodies, attachments, your own address, or emails the rules already handled, and does not store what is sent or returned. Groq handles the data under its own terms and privacy policy, which apply to it. Before anything is sent you are shown a dialog listing exactly this and must agree; until then the switch is off. You can turn it off at any time, and it also turns off when you disconnect Gmail. Entries filed this way are labelled “AI-suggested” in the tracker so you can check or remove them.
  • You can disconnect at any time in the Job Tracker (this revokes the token with Google), or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Nexus OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide the tracker feature you asked for; it is not used for advertising, not sold, and Nexus OS does not use it to train AI models. Apart from the optional AI check described above, which only runs if you turn it on, Gmail data is not transferred to anyone.

3. Other services involved

  • Google — Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Gmail (if connected), and Google Fonts (which sees your IP address when the page loads).
  • Cloudflare — runs the AI service and, if enabled, stores usage counters; also serves some open-source libraries (cdnjs).
  • Groq — generates AI responses. Its own terms and privacy policy apply to what we send it, including, only if you turn on the optional AI email check, the limited email details described in section 2.
  • GitHub — hosts this website. If you connect your GitHub account to push LeetCode solutions, your token stays in your browser and is sent only to api.github.com.
  • Job boards and LeetCode data providers — your browser requests public job listings and public profile data directly from them, so they can see your IP address. No personal data from your portal is sent.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking cookies.

4. What is never synced or shared

Your GitHub token and any Groq API key you enter are stored only in your browser on the device where you typed them. They are excluded from cloud sync.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and Google encrypts Firestore data at rest. Firestore rules restrict each account's data to that account. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password and verify your email.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Export: Settings → Data → Export JSON downloads everything stored in your browser.
  • Delete: Settings → Danger zone → “Delete my account and data” removes your account, your Firestore copy, the copy on that device, and revokes Gmail access. It cannot be undone. Nexus OS cannot reach into other browsers where you used the portal: those devices are signed out, but any copy already stored there stays until you clear that browser's site data.
  • Usage counters expire on their own within two days. We keep no separate backups of your portal data.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Most of this you can do yourself in the portal (sections 6 above); for anything else contact . Under EU/UK law our basis for processing is providing the service you asked for (contract), and your consent for optional features like Gmail, which you can withdraw at any time.

8. Cookies and local storage

Nexus OS uses your browser's local storage and IndexedDB to keep your data, your theme choice and your sign-in session. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Children

Nexus OS is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell signed-in users in the portal.
